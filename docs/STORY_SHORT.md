# The Letter from Paris

## 1. The flight over

Secret code: **BONJOUR**

Viv is a private detective. People come to her with puzzles that nobody else can solve.

One morning a letter comes. The stamp is from France, a country far away across the sea.

> Chère Viv,  
> Someone wants to steal a painting from my museum. I need the best detective in the world. Please come to Paris! Your plane leaves today.  
> Monsieur Noir  
> Director, Musée d'Orsay

*"Chère" means Dear. A museum is a building that keeps famous paintings safe so everyone can see them.*

Paris is the biggest city in France. The people there speak French, and Viv does not know a single word of it.

She opens her notebook. There is just enough time to learn a few words before the plane leaves.

### Notebook: Hello and names

*Hello, goodbye and your name are in your notebook. Next: the airline desk.*

### Talk: At the airline desk

Viv goes to the airport. Her plane belongs to a French airline, so the people at the desk speak French. Time to try out those words!

The airline desk. The agent is typing. She has not looked up.

**Viv:** Bonjour, Madame.

**Airline agent:** Bonjour, Madame ! Comment vous appelez-vous ? *(Hello, ma'am! What is your name?)*

**Viv** *(putting word tiles in order)*: Je m'appelle Viv

**Airline agent:** Très bien. Alors, vous avez des bagages à enregistrer, ou seulement un bagage à main ? *(too fast to follow)*

That was much too fast!

**Viv:** Je ne comprends pas.

**Airline agent:** Oh, pardon, Madame ! *(Oh, sorry, ma'am!)*

**Viv:** Parlez-vous anglais ?

**Airline agent:** Oui, un petit peu ! *(Yes, a little!)*

You tried French first, so the agent is glad to help. She asks again in English, then prints your ticket.

**Airline agent:** Voilà votre billet. Le numéro de la porte est sur le billet. *(Here is your ticket. The gate number is on the ticket.)*

*For her notebook. Which number is your gate? Write it in your notebook. Choices: 22, 7, 14. Answer: 7.*

**Airline agent:** Bon voyage, Madame ! *(Have a good trip, ma'am!)*

**Viv:** Merci, Madame. Au revoir !

**Airline agent:** Au revoir ! *(Goodbye!)*

*You have your ticket. Next: words for the airport.*

### Notebook: Airport words

*Bag, suitcase, ticket, passport. Next: the bag check.*

### Talk: The bag check

Before the gates comes the bag check. Every bag goes through a machine, and a man looks at the pictures. He speaks French too.

The man at the bag check is waiting for you.

**Viv:** Bonjour, Monsieur.

**Bag check:** Bonjour, Madame. Votre sac, s'il vous plaît. *(Hello, ma'am. Your bag, please.)*

*She has to hand over the right thing: the bag, from passport, bag, ticket, croissant.*

**Bag check:** Merci. Et votre billet ? *(Thank you. And your ticket?)*

*She has to hand over the right thing: the ticket, from ticket, loupe, passport.*

**Bag check:** Et la valise rouge ? C'est votre valise ? *(And the red suitcase? Is it your suitcase?)*

He points at a big red suitcase. You only have a bag.

**Viv:** Non, Monsieur.

**Bag check:** Ah, pardon ! Voilà votre sac. Bon voyage ! *(Oh, sorry! Here is your bag. Have a good trip!)*

**Viv:** Merci, Monsieur. Au revoir !

**Bag check:** Au revoir, Madame ! *(Goodbye, ma'am!)*

*Your bag is through. Next: find the gate before the plane leaves.*

### Go!: Find the gate

She has her ticket, but the plane leaves soon. Read the signs and find the gate!

*Hold your finger where you want to go. Read the signs to find your gate.*

1. Find your gate.

2. The door is open. Walk through to the plane!

Viv steps onto the plane just in time.

**Cabin crew:** Bonjour, bienvenue ! *(Hello, welcome!)*

The plane climbs above the clouds. Next stop: Paris. Somewhere down there, a thief is making plans.

*Viv is on her way to Paris.*

## 15. The cheese office

Secret code: **REVOLUTION**

The king of the mice has asked for help. Viv must speak for the mice at the cheese office, where people decide where cheese goes.

But first she must know the cheeses, and where cheese comes from: la vache, le lait, l'herbe, la pluie.

### Notebook: Cheese and the farm

*La vache, le lait, l'herbe, il ne pleut pas. D'abord, puis, après. Next: the cheese office.*

### Talk: The problem

L'Office du fromage. Behind a desk sits Madame Lefèvre, in charge of all the cheese around Paris. The king of the mice hides in Viv's pocket.

*Une poche is a pocket.*

Say hello to Madame Lefèvre.

**Viv:** Bonjour, Madame.

**Madame Lefèvre:** Bonjour. Il y a un problème : il n'y a pas assez de fromage. *(un problème = a problem; pas assez de = not enough)*

**Viv:** Pourquoi ?

**Madame Lefèvre:** D'abord, il ne pleut pas. Puis, il n'y a pas d'herbe. Après, les vaches donnent moins de lait. Alors, il y a moins de fromage. *(donnent = give; moins de = less; alors = so)*

Put the steps in order, the way she tells them.

*Viv puts the steps in order:*

1. D'abord: Il ne pleut pas.
2. Puis: Il n'y a pas d'herbe.
3. Après: Les vaches donnent moins de lait.
4. Alors: Il y a moins de fromage.

**Madame Lefèvre:** Voilà le problème.

*No rain, no grass, less milk, less cheese. Next: words for enough.*

### Notebook: Enough

*Assez, pas assez, trop, besoin. Next: speak up for the mice.*

**Viv:** Non. Les souris sont fâchées contre le roi.

**Madame Lefèvre:** D'accord ! Avec du fromage, tout le monde est content. Aidez-moi à trier le fromage. *(d'accord = OK; aidez-moi = help me; trier = to sort)*
